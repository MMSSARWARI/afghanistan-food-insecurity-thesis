A model from 2023 could not predict 2024

Same 26 provinces and 44 harmonized predictors

The 2023 model placed 61.9% of 2024 households in the Poor group, while the real share was 36.9%. Its area under the curve also fell from 0.881 to 0.633, so the relationships themselves changed. One reason is geography: the provincial Poor shares of the two years have a rank correlation of only 0.49.

About this dashboard

This dashboard summarizes the bachelor's thesis Machine Learning Analysis of Household Food Insecurity and Vulnerability Profiles in Afghanistan by Mohammad Mahdi Sarwari, University of Cassino and Southern Lazio, supervised by Prof. Ciro Russo, academic year 2025/2026.

Shares of households are weighted with the survey weights. Model results come from a test set of 2,374 households. All results describe associations, not causes.

Data. FAO Data in Emergencies Monitoring (DIEM) household surveys for Afghanistan, Rounds 4 to 8. FAO Microdata Catalogue

Code. Four Jupyter notebooks. GitHub repository

Boundaries. Natural Earth, public domain.
